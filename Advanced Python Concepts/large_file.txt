Manchester City F.C. faces a historic turning point following a landmark ruling by an independent commission finding the club guilty of 114 out of 115 Premier League financial regulations.

Overview of the Financial Verdict

• The Charges: The independent commission found Manchester City guilty of serious financial breaches covering a nine-season span from 2009–10 through 2017–18.
• The Core Findings: The club was found to have arranged "sham" commercial contracts to artificially inflate revenues by over £900 million, hid true financial records from auditors, and disguised player and managerial compensation (including with former manager Roberto Mancini).
• Club Response: Manchester City maintains its innocence, calling the ruling "unsafe" and stating it contains material errors of law and fact. The club has formally lodged an appeal to be heard by a separate independent appeal board. Full details of the commission's breakdown are documented via The Athletic and official statements on The Guardian.
